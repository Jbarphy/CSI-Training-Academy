* User information is contained within the HKEY_CURRENT_USER with NTUSER.DAT and USRCLASS.DAT
* User programs logs are found in Amcache Hive
	* AppCompat\Programs\Amcache.hve
* Transaction log and backups give important info on writing data to hives that are potentially not saved to the hives but applied to the registry
* UserAssist records usage of applications by individual users
* ShimCache records information about executed appliations and their associated DLLs
* BAM and DAM are management of applications and deices on Window's Systems' 
	* BAM for background tasks
	* DAM for device applications
