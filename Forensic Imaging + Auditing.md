* Artifacts are the evidence that points to an activity in order to support a hypothesis or claim.
* NIST method describes process in form of:
* Preparation -> Detection and Analysis -> Containment, Eradication, and Recovery, Post-incident Recovery
	* SANS also has similar process
* Imaging allows examination of deleted files, hidden files, and unallocated space through an imaged copy
	* Process, Environment for imaging, Raw image creation
	* Integrity Check of an image
* Audit trail provides chronological record of steps and actions within a system that can provide accountability and security for the process of imaging
